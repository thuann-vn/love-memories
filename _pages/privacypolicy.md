# Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Love Memories app for iOS and Android.

## What the app stores

The names, birthdays, start date, photos, notes and settings you enter are saved only on your phone. The app has no account and no server of its own, and it does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

Photos you pick are copied into the app's own folder on your phone. The app only reads the photos you choose.

## What the app does not use

The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not track you across apps or websites.

## Network

The app downloads sample wallpapers and videos from our storage server, and checks for app updates. These requests carry no personal information.

## Purchases

Love Memories Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we do not receive your payment details.

## Notifications

Milestone and birthday reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification data leaves the device.

## Deleting your data

Delete a moment in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
